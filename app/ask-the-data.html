Ask the data

Run SQL on the map’s public data file, inside your own browser. Nothing you type leaves this page.

What this is

A small database engine (DuckDB, compiled to WebAssembly) that loads data/venues.json, the same file the map reads. It is the published export, not the full warehouse: no lineage, no raw sources, and no phone numbers or other private fields, because those are not in the file. The tables are described in the data dictionary.

The engine is about 36 MB (35.7 MB for the WebAssembly file, uncompressed), so it loads only when you press the button. It is served from this site, not a CDN. Version 1.29.0; files and checksums are in app/vendor/duckdb.

    Read these with care

    • These are counts of what the export lists, not of every court in the two states. Many pins come from OpenStreetMap, where court counts and names are often missing; the “how sure are the names” question shows how many.
    • A court count of 0 means unknown, not none. A venue that lists a sport is not proof that the sport can be booked or played there today.
    • The export is rebuilt after each refresh; its date is shown once the data loads.
    • Needs a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Not tested on phones; the engine file is large for a mobile connection.
    This page sets no cookies and requests nothing from other sites. Pressing the button downloads the engine and data/venues.json from this site; your queries run on your device and are not sent anywhere.

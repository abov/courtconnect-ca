    About CourtConnect

    One free map of every racket sport in California and Nevada: tennis, pickleball, padel, squash, badminton, table tennis and the lesser-known ones, on public parks, hitting walls, rec centers and private clubs. Each place says how sure we are (confirmed, official or reported) and when it was last checked. We link out to the venue's own page; we never take bookings or payments, and there are no accounts.

    For players

    Find a court, see what is on to watch, and find groups to play with.

    For clubs and facilities

    Is your club missing or wrong? Tell us with a link to your public page; we review it and update the listing when the page backs it up. Fix or add your listing (no sign-in)

    For companies and organizers

    Running a tournament, league or open play? Send us the dates and your public page; we review it and list it as an event when the page backs it up. Add an event (no sign-in)

    A listing or label changes only with public evidence. It cannot be bought, and there is no paid or featured placement.

# CourtConnect data dictionary

File: `data/venues.json` (generated by `ingestion/export_app_data.py`; the docstring there is the source of truth). Top level: `meta`, `sports`, `venues`, `events`, `social`, `walls`, `platforms`. All keys are short on purpose. Missing optional keys mean "not known", never "no".

## meta
`generated` (date), `venues`, `named`, `confirmed`, `sports_total`, `sports_with_venues`, `events`, `social`, `walls`, `link_outs` (counts), `attribution`.

## sports
Array; a sport's **index** is its id everywhere else (`venues[].p[][0]`, `platforms[].sp`, `sports[].b`).
`n` name, `v` venues found, `s` coverage status, `r` recommended next data source, `d` short blurb, `f` facts {`p` players, `c` court size, `g` gear, `o` governing body, `y` year, `ou` official site}, `u` links {`l` learn more, `r` rules, `c` court, `g` gear}, `w`/`wn` = played anywhere (where / note, no fixed court), `b` = index list of the court sports this one shares (not counted twice).

## venues
`i` id, `n` name (empty = unnamed), `y` lat, `x` lon, `c` city, `r` region (CA/NV), `a` street address, `w` website (only if working), `o` offerings string, `f` venue bits, `p` sports at the venue, `ac` link-outs, `ru` rules/notes (hand-curated), `xu` 1 = public access not confirmed.

- `q` name quality: `c` confirmed court/operator name or hand-curated, `p` business listing, `i` inferred from nearby park/school, `u` unnamed. When several `i` pins in one city share a name, `n` ends in a suffix such as `(north courts, 6 courts)` (or `, #2` if still equal): it is computed from the pin's position relative to the group and its mapped court count, not from any source, and `q` stays `i`.
- `s` source: `o` OpenStreetMap, `p` places directory (Overture), `b` both, `m` hand-curated.
- `f` bits: 1 lit, 2 public, 4 free, 8 members-only. Test with bitwise AND (`f & 2` = public).
- `p` = `[[sportIndex, courts, surfaceMask], ...]`. surfaceMask bits: 1 indoor, 2 outdoor, 4 sand, 8 clay, 16 grass, 32 hard, 64 turf. `courts` 0 = count unknown.
- `o` offering letters: O open play, C clinics, L private lessons, G group classes, D drop-in, T tournaments, E leagues, S social events.
- `ac` = `[type, title, url, provider, start, end, price]` (strings; empty = not given). Type is one of open_play, clinic, private_lesson, group_class, drop_in, tournament, league, social_event, club_page.
- Confirmation `cf`: `v` visited in person (`cd` = date), `o` stated by the operator, `r` reported only. `cn` / `rn` = number of different people who confirmed / reported. Absent = from map data only.
- Inferred address: `ai` 1 + `ad` metres = nearest address point; `ap` 1 = address of the enclosing park/school/club; `ns` = nearest road ("Near <street>"); `al` = other listed names at the same address (merged duplicates).
- Not open yet: `up` badge text ("Reported: opening soon" with `ul` 1 = only a directory or press listing says so), `ud` = date checked.

## events (to watch)
`n` name, `sp` sport name, `l` level (pro, amateur, junior, college, exhibition), `f` watch format, `v` venue, `c` city, `y`/`x`, `pr` location precision, `s`/`e` start/end date, `d` date status, `t` ticket info, `u` info link, `k` tickets link, `vo` verified on. Every event links out to its organizer.

## social (recurring get-togethers)
`n` series name, `sp` sport name, `k` kind, `o` organizer, `v` venue, `c` city, `y`/`x`, `pr` precision, `sc` schedule (organizer's wording), `sk` how it was found, `u` info link, `g` signup link, `vo` verified on. Optional: `ll` last listed, `sl` skill level, `au` audience, `ag` age focus, `ct` courts, `pi` price info. Optional keys appear only when the organizer states them.

## walls (hitting walls for solo practice)
`n`, `c`, `r`, `y`/`x`, `pr` precision, `t` wall types, `sp` sport names suited, `nv` near venue, `su` surface, `l` lit 1/0, `f` free 1/0, `ac` access, `cf` confirmation, `d` reported on, `ev` evidence, `wn` walls at this pin (absent = 1). Missing `l`/`f` = unknown.

## platforms (player-matching apps)
`n`, `sp` [sport indexes], `d` what it does, `p` price note, `u` link, `vo` verified on. Link-outs only; CourtConnect has no accounts or matching.

## Answering well
- To find courts for a sport: look up its index in `sports`, then filter `venues` where any `p[i][0]` equals it; compute distance from `y`/`x`.
- Say what is inferred. Use `q`, `cf`, `ai`/`ap`/`ns` and `xu`; do not turn guesses into facts.
- Link to the venue website `w` or its `ac` link-outs; never invent hours, prices or booking details.
- Phone numbers and Google IDs are deliberately not in the file.
- Attribution: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), Overture Maps Foundation, plus hand-curated entries.

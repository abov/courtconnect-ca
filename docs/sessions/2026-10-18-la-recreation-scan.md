# 2026-10-18 City of LA Recreation and Parks scan (tennis, handball, paddle, racquetball)
`recreation.parks.lacity.gov` (403 to scripts) lists "Facility Features" on every park / rec-centre page and the sitemap has ~400 of them. `ingestion/la_recreation_scan.js` (run in the browser pane, same-origin fetch, 0.4 s per request, ~3 min, 0 errors) saves `ingestion/data/la_recreation_scan.csv` (85 pages with a racket feature); `ingestion/la_recreation_scan.py` joins coordinates from the city's open-data list (`ukgg-rgf9`) and lists what the map lacks. Seeded: 11 handball sites into `manual_walls` (`wall_type=other`: the pages never give a wall count or court count, so they never count as 1-wall or 3-wall; lit/unlit from the page, access blank), 4 tennis pins (Algin Sutton, Baldwin Hills, El Sereno, Lafayette) and Yosemite racquetball into `manual_venues` (points are the rec centre, flagged approximate).
Not done: 8 pickleball gaps from the same scan (outside the ask); Del Rey paddle tennis (no coordinates, West LA pop tennis is in open PRs); wall type for the 11 handball sites (next: Esri aerial via `imagery_crop.py`, 1-wall slab about 20 x 34 ft vs three walls). Outside LA no shared format exists (Socrata only LA/Oakland/Berkeley; sitemaps of SF, Sacramento, Long Beach, Santa Monica, Clark, Washoe carry no amenity lists; San Jose/Henderson/Anaheim 403): try SF Rec & Park and City of San Diego park pages next.

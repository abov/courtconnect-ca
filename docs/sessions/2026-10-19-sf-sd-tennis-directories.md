# 2026-10-19 SF Rec & Park and City of San Diego tennis directories seeded
`ingestion/city_tennis_directories.py` reads the two one-page directories (SF `sfrecpark.org/1446/Tennis-Court-Directory`: 64 facilities; San Diego `sandiego.gov/.../sports/tenniscourts`: 31 facilities), geocodes each park once with Nominatim (cached in `ingestion/data/city_tennis_geocode.csv`, backs off on 429 and timeouts) and links it to the mapped Tennis pin its point lands on (name overlap within 600 m, or the same court count within 250 m; `PIN_HINTS` for hand-checked cases such as Mission -> Mission Playground). 81 `manual_venues` rows (`sf-rec-*`, `sd-city-*`), all merged onto existing pins (no new pins): official court count, access `public`, SF reservable vs walk-up split ($5 per court-hour, rec.us/sfrecpark; walk-up rules link), San Diego hours, evidence = directory URL + read date; confirmed venues 1,439 -> 1,496.
Not seeded: 7 facilities that already have hand-made rows (La Jolla, Morley Field, Lake Murray, Pacific Beach Tennis Club, Carmel Valley, Standley, Tierrasanta); 5 held for review because a different pin sits close or the geocode failed (SF Moscone vs 'SF Tennis Lessons' 60 m, Peixotto 15th St vs 'South Corona Heights' 89 m, States Street (Flint St) no geocode; SD MLK Jr Rec Center vs 'Mountain View Sports & Racquet' 295 m, Robb Athletic Field vs 'Peninsula Tennis Club' 149 m); McLaren (6 courts) and 'Sunset' (shares the West Sunset pin) dropped (McLaren's pin is not a merge target for the manual row and made a duplicate). Court counts are max(OSM, official), so an official count cannot lower an inflated OSM count (Alice Marble 4 vs 5, Balboa SF 4 vs 12). SF pickleball directory (`/1772/`, 15 outdoor + 7 indoor sites) not done. Re-run the script at each sync.

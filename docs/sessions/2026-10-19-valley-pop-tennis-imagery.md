# 2026-10-19 San Fernando Valley pop tennis imagery search (no new courts found)
Pulled every OSM racket-sport pitch polygon in the valley (bbox 34.12,-118.82,34.33,-118.25; 1,400 shapes), measured each one's rectangle, and kept the 125 that fit a 20 x 44 ft court (the known Van Nuys Sherman Oaks pair measures 6.4 x 15.5 m in OSM). They cluster into 55 sites; 9 were private-tagged and skipped, and I viewed the other 46 on Esri z19/z20 (viewing only, nothing stored). Result: 0 new confirmed pop tennis courts. Lone shapes are private backyards; the multi-court sites (El Cariso, Braemar, Rancho Simi, Rancho Tapo, Simi school pads, Sherman Oaks and Sylmar pairs) show pickleball no-volley zones. Only ambiguous one: El Caballero Country Club (34.1561,-118.5361), a narrow tennis-style court at a private club, too blurry to call.
Why size is not enough: pickleball and paddle tennis share the 20 x 44 ft footprint, so the line pattern decides (no-volley band 7 ft from the net vs service lines 12 ft). The OSM-first method cannot find courts nobody has mapped. Map has 1 valley pop tennis pin (Van Nuys Sherman Oaks); the city's own feature list for that park omits paddle tennis (only its fee text names it), so city pages under-report it.
Next (needs owner OK, not started): (a) view the 113 public tennis sites with 2+ courts (198 non-private in all) at z20 for unmapped narrow fenced pads beside them, about 28 contact sheets; (b) read the fee/notes text of the LA Recreation and Parks tennis facility pages for "Paddle Tennis" (Browser pane, the site 403s scripts); (c) owner sightings. NAIP (0.6 m) is too coarse for these courts (about 10 x 22 px).
Follow-up, same day: the owner confirmed El Caballero Country Club has only tennis and pickleball (no pop tennis), so it is closed as a candidate. Re-scanned all 402 LA Recreation and Parks park / rec center / tennis-facility pages for the word "paddle" in the full page text (the 2026-10-18 scan only kept the Facility Features list): only Jim Gilliam and Bill Rosendahl Del Rey list "Paddle Tennis" as a feature (both outside the valley, both already known); 9 pay-tennis pages carry the generic fee line "Paddle Tennis/Pickleball Court $14.00 per hour" (Balboa, Poinsettia, Cheviot Hills, Westchester, Vermont Canyon, Van Nuys Sherman Oaks, Riverside, Westwood, Arthur Ashe), which is a rate card, not proof of a paddle court (VNSO is the only one confirmed). Balboa Tennis Center aerial (z20): tennis courts only. Lake Balboa Tennis Center (34.1891,-118.5033): a blue pad with 2 small courts just south of the tennis courts, sport unreadable from the aerial; owner to check on Google Maps.
Correction: the Lake Balboa spot (34.1888,-118.5033) is inside Birmingham Community Charter High School (17000 Haynes St, OSM school area), not a public park; "Lake Balboa Tennis Center" is only our pin name for the school's courts. The owner looked on Google Maps (2026-10-19) and could not tell what the 2-court pad is; unresolved, will recheck later. Nothing seeded.
Cheap checks, same day: a name search of OSM and Overture (valley bbox) for paddle / pop tennis / platform tennis finds only the two Simi Valley OSM shapes already viewed; three standard web searches (valley parks, Burbank/Glendale/Calabasas/Woodland Hills/Northridge, a paddle tennis association list) surfaced only Van Nuys Sherman Oaks and Venice Beach, both already on the map (Pura Padel in Sherman Oaks is padel, a different sport). So the only remaining route to unmapped public courts is the imagery pass over the 113 public tennis sites, or owner sightings.
Imagery pass over the tennis sites (done, same day): 112 non-private OSM tennis sites with 2+ courts across the valley (Westlake to Sunland; Van Nuys Sherman Oaks excluded), viewed on Esri z20 (z19 west of Calabasas; capture Jan 2026 at Lake Balboa), 19 contact sheets. Result: no new pop tennis court. Almost all are full tennis courts; small-court pads were pickleball (El Cariso, Braemar, the pad at 34.212,-118.340 whose lines measure 15.5/14/14.5 ft baseline-to-kitchen-to-net, i.e. pickleball, not 10/12/12/10 ft paddle tennis) or private backyards (Encino/Tarzana/Reseda lots, never added). Inconclusive: Calabasas Swim and Tennis Center (34.1554,-118.6384, 2 small courts, members-only, shadowed) and Calabasas Golf and Country Club (34.1449,-118.6650, 2 small courts, z19 blur); Birmingham Community Charter High School pad still waits for the owner. Unconfirmed, not pop tennis: the Esri aerial at Sepulveda Recreation Center (34.2287,-118.4579) shows two long white strips about 8 m apart beside the tennis courts that might be walls; the owner does not see them on Google Maps, so treat as nothing until a visit report says otherwise. Limits: OSM only draws courts mappers saw, west-valley imagery is blurry, and an unmapped pad that is not beside a mapped tennis court was not searched.
Owner review (2026-10-19): sees no pop tennis at Sepulveda Recreation Center (the white strips seen on the aerial are unconfirmed and not recorded anywhere else) and doubts the two Calabasas club pads are pop tennis; all three are closed as pop tennis candidates, no seed change. Search finished: the valley's only confirmed public pop tennis stays Van Nuys Sherman Oaks (plus the owner's private home courts, left off the map). Birmingham High pad is the one open item.

# Coverage snapshot (2026-11-10)

Covered regions: California (CA), Nevada (NV), Oregon (OR), Washington (WA), Hawaii (HI), Arizona (AZ), Mexico (MX) (ADR 0014, 0028). Venues by region: AZ 2,090, CA 10,084, HI 452, MX 530, NV 547, OR 1,171, WA 2,076.

Generated by `ingestion/export_coverage_report.py` from `marts.mart_sport_gaps`. Live numbers: query that table in DuckDB.

**42,783 OSM court/facility records plus Overture places -> 16,950 venues. 7,782 venues have a name; 1,514 of those are confirmed (tagged on the court, its operator, or an Overture place) and the rest are inferred from the park, school or club the court sits inside. Overture places matched 892 OSM venues and added 556 venues OpenStreetMap had no court for. 398 of the venues are hitting walls that stand at no listed court venue (ADR 0032).**

Sports by status: **covered** 39, **sparse** 4, **tagged_none_found** 3, **not_found** 36.

`possible_only` means no confirmed venue, but some business names mention the sport (weak matches, never counted as venues). Review them in `marts.mart_places_to_review` and confirm real ones in `transform/seeds/place_sport_overrides.csv`.

## Surfaces and indoor/outdoor: what the data can and cannot say

Court records by playing surface: unknown 37,747, hard 3,900, clay 122, turf 94, grass 21, other 20, sand 3.

Court records by setting: likely_outdoor 41,207, unknown 787, outdoor 652, indoor 125, covered 12.

**Read this before using the surface or indoor filters.**
- Most OpenStreetMap courts have **no surface tag**, so "unknown" is the biggest bucket. Clay, grass and sand courts exist,
  but only a handful are tagged as such.
- `likely_outdoor` means the court is an untagged outdoor pitch. It is an assumption, not data. Only `outdoor`, `indoor` and
  `covered` come from an explicit tag.
- Indoor courts are mostly mapped as buildings or sports centres, not as individual courts, so indoor court counts are low.
- Beach / sand courts are almost unmapped (a few courts statewide). Beach tennis needs another source.

Filling these gaps needs a second source (Google Places, club and league directories). See `docs/decisions/0004`.

# CalNeva coverage snapshot (2026-10-18)

Covered regions: California and Nevada (ADR 0014). Venues by region: CA 9,710, NV 547.

Generated by `ingestion/export_coverage_report.py` from `marts.mart_sport_gaps`. Live numbers: query that table (DuckDB, or Snowflake `MARTS.MART_SPORT_GAPS`).

**27,547 OSM court/facility records plus Overture places -> 10,257 venues. 4,483 venues have a name; 1,047 of those are confirmed (tagged on the court, its operator, or an Overture place) and the rest are inferred from the park, school or club the court sits inside. Overture places matched 621 OSM venues and added 386 venues OpenStreetMap had no court for.**

Sports by status: **covered** 21, **sparse** 1, **tagged_none_found** 8, **not_found** 46.

`possible_only` means no confirmed venue, but some business names mention the sport (weak matches, never counted as venues). Review them in `marts.mart_places_to_review` and confirm real ones in `transform/seeds/place_sport_overrides.csv`.

## Surfaces and indoor/outdoor: what the data can and cannot say

Court records by playing surface: unknown 24,606, hard 2,402, turf 83, clay 67, other 14, grass 12, sand 3.

Court records by setting: likely_outdoor 26,597, outdoor 577, unknown 314, indoor 48, covered 11.

**Read this before using the surface or indoor filters.**
- Most OpenStreetMap courts have **no surface tag**, so "unknown" is the biggest bucket. Clay, grass and sand courts exist,
  but only a handful are tagged as such.
- `likely_outdoor` means the court is an untagged outdoor pitch. It is an assumption, not data. Only `outdoor`, `indoor` and
  `covered` come from an explicit tag.
- Indoor courts are mostly mapped as buildings or sports centres, not as individual courts, so indoor court counts are low.
- Beach / sand courts are almost unmapped (a few courts statewide). Beach tennis and sandball need another source.

Filling these gaps needs a second source (Google Places, club and league directories). See `docs/decisions/0004`.

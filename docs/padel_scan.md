# Padel court check (plan, added 2026-10-17)

## Why
Padel and pop tennis (also called paddle tennis, `Pop Tennis` in the sheet) are two separate sports on two different courts, but the names get mixed up:
- **Padel**: enclosed court 20 x 10 m (65 x 32 ft) with glass and/or mesh walls that stay in play, turf surface, solid stringless racket, depressurised tennis ball. Sheet row `Padel`, FIP.
- **Pop tennis**: open court 50 x 20 ft (60 x 27 ft on the sheet) with a low net, no walls, concrete, solid paddle, felt rubber ball. Sheet row `Pop Tennis`, IPTA. Common in West LA / Southern California parks.
- **Platform tennis** is a third, separate sport (aluminium deck with wire screens, outdoors in winter). Do not merge it into either.

Known mix-ups already fixed one by one: 10 OSM courts tagged `sport=padel` that were pop tennis (El Segundo, Fox Hills, Mindanao Way, Jim Gilliam, Westchester; `osm_court_sport_overrides.csv`), a Padel pin on a Miami Beach club (`website_overrides`), and Overture category/name conflicts (`assert_category_sport_not_contradicted_by_name`). The cause is systemic (OSM taggers use `padel` or `paddle_tennis` loosely; "paddle" appears in both names), so check the whole set instead of waiting for reports.

## Reference sites (leads only)
Use these to find club names we may be missing; they are compiled directories, so treat each entry as a lead, not as evidence.
- https://www.padelcourtsfinder.com/
- https://padelusa.org/where-to-play/ (USPA)
- https://www.padelmapusa.com/

Rules: public pages only (ADR 0007); read each site's terms before copying anything beyond facts (venue name, city, number of courts, link); do not bulk-copy or scrape their lists; no Google Places data (ADR 0005). Confirm every venue on the club's own page or a public booking page (read as text, never the owner's logins) and keep `verified_on` + `evidence`. Add a row to `docs/sport_directories.md` for each site once its terms are read.

## Steps (cheapest first; one per session)
1. **Audit what we have.** List every venue with Padel in the export (`venues.json`/`dim_venue`) and every Pop Tennis venue; flag Padel rows that are OSM-only or Overture-only with no club page, and Padel rows whose name or park looks like a rec centre or school (likely pop tennis). Write the list to the session note; no seed changes yet.
2. **Fix the mix-ups.** For each flagged row: confirm on a public page, then reject Padel (and add Pop Tennis if the page says so) in `osm_court_sport_overrides.csv` / `place_sport_overrides.csv`, or confirm Padel in `place_sport_overrides.csv`. Run `dbt build --select` on the touched models first, full build once at the end.
3. **Add missing padel clubs.** One metro at a time (LA, San Diego, Bay Area, Las Vegas, Sacramento), compare the three reference sites against our list; for each club we lack, read its own page and add it to `manual_venues.csv` (court count in the evidence; indoor/outdoor if stated). Cheapest first: Los Angeles, then propose more.
4. **Guard rails.** Add a dbt test or a `ci/` check so a Padel row needs one of: OSM `sport=padel` on a court that also has `walls`/`glass` tags or a club name, an Overture place with "padel" in the name, or a confirmed override. The only real OSM keys are `padel` (-> Padel) and `paddle_tennis`, `paddletennis`, `pop_tennis`, `poptennis` (-> Pop Tennis); `padel_tennis` does not exist and was removed 2026-10-17. `padel` is now `medium` (audit: most aerial-imagery padel tags were wrong), so a Padel row stays in the review queue until a club page or override confirms it. Optionally add "pop tennis" / "paddle tennis" / "padel" alias checks to `sport_aliases.csv` so a search for one never returns the other.
5. **Tell people the difference.** A one-line note in the sport "About" card (see `docs/sport_discovery_plan.md`) and in the suggest-a-change form: "Padel = walled glass court; pop tennis = open court with a low net". The blurb itself goes in the Google Sheet.
6. **OSM.** Where an OSM court is clearly pop tennis but tagged padel (or the reverse), fix it in OSM (`docs/contribute_to_osm.md`) so the next extract is right without an override.

## Rules learned in the 2026-10-17 audit
- **Operator is not venue.** A company that runs padel courts under its own brand (Taktika Padel) is not the place. The venue is where the courts physically are (LA Galaxy Park, Barnes Tennis Center, the University of the Pacific complex, Shadow Mountain Resort); the operator goes in `rules` and its booking / open-play links go in `venue_actions.csv` on that venue. Where a company and a place share one brand (Padel Up, Bay Padel, Park Padel), the brand location is the venue. Never credit an operator at a place that does not list it (Fairmont Grand Del Mar is not a Taktika site).
- **Aerial-photo tags are guesses.** OSM `padel` tags added from aerial imagery (MapRoulette passes, "add sports courts") were mostly wrong; `padel` is `medium` confidence and a Padel pin needs a club page, a confirmed override or a size match on imagery.
- **Reject with a replacement.** `osm_court_sport_overrides.csv` has an optional `correct_sport`: a court whose tag is wrong but whose real sport is known (pickleball at Bakersfield CC, San Juan School, Lake Forest, Orangetree Park, Silver Creek, iTennis La Habra) counts for that sport at `osm_tag_loose` strength (needs review) instead of disappearing.
- **Imagery check (size test).** `ingestion/imagery_crop.py` fetches an Esri World Imagery crop with true-scale outlines (padel 10 x 20 m, pickleball / paddle tennis 6 x 13 m, tennis 11 x 24 m). Glass walls are not visible from above and NAIP (0.6 m) is too coarse, so judge by size, quote the imagery date in `evidence`, never commit the images. It proves size, not sport: a 6 x 13 m court is pickleball OR paddle tennis, so pair it with a page or the owner's report. The owner also checks on Google Maps; their reports count as `reported`, but the project does not pull or store Google imagery or Places data (ADR 0005).
- **Not open yet is still shown.** A venue whose own page says it is coming soon goes in `manual_venues.csv` plus one row in `venue_opening_status.csv`; the map draws a hollow ring, an "Opening soon" badge and a caveat (court counts, address and date can change; the pin may be approximate). A directory- or press-only claim is shown as a lighter 'Reported: opening soon' pin (owner request) and also stays in `venue_leads.csv` until an official page confirms it. When it opens, delete the status row and update the venue (courts, address). Same rule for beach tennis and pop tennis; none found coming in CA/NV on 2026-10-17.
- **Directories are leads.** padelcourtsfinder.com (terms: cite with credit, no wholesale scraping), padelusa.org (the old /where-to-play URL is gone; clubs are under /clubs/), padelmapusa.com and padelbrowser.com were read for names and cities only. One lists Venice Beach paddle tennis as padel, so every club was confirmed on its own page or kept as a lead in `venue_leads.csv`.
- **PlayByPoint club pages are a lead source (owner 2026-11-05).** Check a club's public PlayByPoint page as a visitor, one club at a time, when a lead or an existing pin names it (ADR 0006: `robots.txt` blocks only reservations and user pages). Store the club's name, city, court count and the link, as published; no bulk crawl, no search-result scraping, no logins (ADR 0007). A club found only there stays in `venue_leads.csv` until its own page or a report confirms it.

## Done when
- Every Padel pin has a club page, a confirmed override, or is labelled inferred; no pop tennis court is shown as Padel (and the reverse).
- The padel count by metro is compared with the three reference sites and the gaps are either added or listed with a reason.
- A test fails when a Padel row has no supporting signal (`assert_padel_has_supporting_signal.sql`, #211). Pop Tennis has its own (`assert_pop_tennis_has_supporting_signal.sql`: rungs 1-2 and 5-6 of `mart_sport_evidence`, a loose OSM tag only while flagged needs_review, never a high-confidence OSM tag or a derived row).

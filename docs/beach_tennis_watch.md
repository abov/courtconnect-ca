# Beach tennis watch list (Santa Monica, Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach)

Added 2026-10-19 at the owner's request: keep an eye on the LA beach tennis organizers, which advertise tournaments on their own sites and Instagram before any governing-body calendar does. Read at every tournament refresh (`docs/tournament_refresh.md`, about every 2 weeks); public pages only, no logins (ADR 0007, ADR 0009 and its 2026-10-19 amendment).

Courts used: Santa Monica Beach at Tower 26 (pins "Beach Tennis Santa Monica" and "OLA Beach Tennis"), Manhattan Beach 7th St, Hermosa Beach 14th St.

## Rules
- Add an event only with a stated year and date; use the organizer's own page over any post or aggregator and note disagreements in `source_note`.
- Level: ITF BT10 / BT50 are `pro`; club and community events are `amateur`. League seasons and league finals days are not events here (note them in the session note).
- Past events (the Sept 20 and Oct 18 BT10s, the July Santa Monica Open) are not added.
